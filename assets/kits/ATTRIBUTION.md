# Factory kit sound credits

The factory Drum Rack kits use the hardware recordings Tunekit embeds
(`assets/drums` at Tunekit revision `424499276b8a5877f202118a1ead6ae50b0aa4d3`),
written out as mono 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAV files with no other change.

[Zynthian TR808-fischer project](https://github.com/zynthian/TR808-fischer).

## 909

Recordings by **Janne G:son Berg (2005)**, edited and uploaded by **altemark**
([TR-909 JGB pack](https://freesound.org/people/altemark/packs/1643/)),
under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/);
the full text is in `909/CC-BY-4.0.txt`, and each file's source page,
original name and checksum in `909/sources.json`. The kit's middle toms are
the low and high tom recordings retuned on their pads.

Suggested credit when distributing music containing these 909 recordings:
"TR-909 samples by Janne G:son Berg / altemark, CC BY 4.0,
https://freesound.org/people/altemark/packs/1643/."

`909/crash.wav` derives from `CRASH.wav` in
[Matthew Dolan's Detroit-909](https://github.com/mdolancode/Detroit-909)
(revision `15891f6a116b26bd7e65c647443d6188e2687c93`), copyright (c) 2021
Matthew Dolan, MIT license, preserved as `909/DETROIT-LICENSE`. Its author
describes self-recorded equipment emulating the TR-909, so it is not claimed
to be an original TR-909 capture.

These asset licenses and credits are in addition to Vibez's GPL-3.0 code
license.
